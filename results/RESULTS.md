# Results

Synthetic data only, generated by `make bench` (`python -m ixrebal.cli bench --out results`).
One universe (seed 7, 500 names, 13 years of daily returns), quarterly reviews 8-51.
Development: reviews 8-31; held out: reviews 32-51, with three disclosed exceptions: held-out reviews were seen in a prototype and in a first full run before the tie rule was fixed (see docs/methodology.md and the sensitivity table at the end).
